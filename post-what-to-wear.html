What to wear to your portrait session

The best outfit is the one that makes you forget about your outfit. Here's what helps.

Choose comfort first

If you're adjusting a collar all session, it shows. Pick clothes you already move well in.

Keep colours calm

Solid, muted colours photograph beautifully. Avoid tiny patterns and large logos, which pull attention from your face.

Bring a second option

A change of top or a jacket gives us a different look with almost no extra time.
